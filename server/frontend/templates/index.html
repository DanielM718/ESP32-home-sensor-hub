Resolution and retention

Raw readings preserve individual samples while retained. Active Monitoring can export raw samples or calculate 1-minute, 5-minute, 15-minute, and 1-hour arithmetic means from that bounded raw interval. Battery voltage uses only battery-valid samples; status flags are never averaged.

The permanent SEN66 tier stores UTC-aligned 15-minute summaries for long-range charts and historical 15-minute exports. Normal charts label stored means as the ordinary measurement and omit max, p95, and event datasets.
